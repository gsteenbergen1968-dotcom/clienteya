# The Law

> **Never build what is not the next logical step.**

---

## Purpose

The Third Law protects the architectural integrity of ClienteYA.

Engineering is not the process of building everything that is possible.

It is the discipline of building only what the architecture is ready to support.

Every unnecessary step introduces unnecessary complexity.

Every unnecessary dependency reduces long-term clarity.

The Third Law exists to ensure that growth strengthens the architecture instead of weakening it.

---

## Rationale

Progress is often mistaken for movement.

Building more features.

Writing more code.

Adding more systems.

These activities create the appearance of progress.

Architecture measures progress differently.

Real progress occurs when every completed step naturally enables the next one.

The next logical step is therefore not determined by urgency.

It is not determined by excitement.

It is determined by the architecture.

When the architecture is respected, complexity remains manageable.

When the architecture is ignored, complexity compounds faster than it can be understood.

---

## Application

Whenever several improvements appear valuable, resist the temptation to pursue them all.

Instead, ask:

> **Which improvement does the architecture require next?**

Only that improvement deserves to be built now.

Everything else remains valuable.

Just not yet.

Architectural discipline is often demonstrated not by what is built...

but by what is deliberately postponed.

---

## Reflection

Patience is not the opposite of progress.

It is often the condition that makes sustainable progress possible.

Before beginning any new work, ask yourself:

> **Am I building because this is the next logical step...**

> **...or simply because it is possible?**

If the answer is uncertain...

return to the architecture.

The next logical step will always reveal itself.
