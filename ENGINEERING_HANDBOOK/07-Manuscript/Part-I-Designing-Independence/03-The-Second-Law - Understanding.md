# The Law

> **Never build what you do not yet understand.**

---

## Purpose

The Second Law protects the quality of every decision made within ClienteYA.

Implementation is never the beginning of engineering.

Understanding is.

Software can only become as good as the understanding on which it is built.

When understanding is incomplete, implementation merely accelerates mistakes.

The purpose of the Second Law is therefore simple:

Understand first.

Build second.

---

## Rationale

Modern engineering makes it possible to build almost anything.

That ability creates a new responsibility.

Not every problem should be solved immediately.

Not every idea should become a feature.

Not every request should become code.

The first responsibility of an engineer is not implementation.

It is understanding.

Understanding the problem.

Understanding the purpose.

Understanding the principles.

Understanding the long-term consequences.

Only when understanding becomes clear should implementation begin.

---

## Application

The Second Law applies whenever uncertainty exists.

If the problem is unclear...

understand it.

If the purpose is unclear...

understand it.

If the architecture is unclear...

understand it.

If the consequences are unclear...

understand them.

Implementation should never be used to discover what should have been understood beforehand.

Building is not a substitute for thinking.

---

## Reflection

Knowledge explains how something works.

Understanding explains why it should exist.

Before building, ask yourself:

> **Do I truly understand what I am about to build?**

If the answer is uncertain...

continue learning.

Not coding.
