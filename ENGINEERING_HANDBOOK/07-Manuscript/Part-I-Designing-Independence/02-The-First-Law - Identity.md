# The Law

> **Never build without first asking:**
>
> **"Is this still ClienteYA?"**

---

## Purpose

The First Law protects the identity of ClienteYA.

Every organisation evolves.

Products improve.

Technology changes.

Markets shift.

Identity must remain recognisable.

Without a stable identity, every improvement becomes a potential source of inconsistency.

The First Law ensures that every meaningful decision strengthens ClienteYA instead of slowly changing it into something else.

---

## Rationale

Engineering is a continuous series of decisions.

Some are large.

Most are small.

Over time, small decisions shape an organisation far more than occasional large ones.

The greatest threat to a strong architecture is rarely a single wrong decision.

It is hundreds of small decisions that individually appear reasonable but collectively move the organisation away from its principles.

The First Law exists to prevent this gradual erosion.

Every meaningful decision must first answer one question:

> **Is this still ClienteYA?**

Only after that question has been answered should implementation begin.

---

## Application

The First Law applies to every meaningful decision within ClienteYA.

Before writing code.

Before introducing a new feature.

Before changing architecture.

Before redesigning an interface.

Before creating a new process.

Before adopting new technology.

Before expanding the organisation.

The question remains the same.

> **Is this still ClienteYA?**

If the answer is yes, continue.

If the answer is no, stop.

If the answer is uncertain, return to the principles before proceeding.

---

## Reflection

Every engineer contributes to the future identity of ClienteYA.

Before continuing, ask yourself:

> **Will this decision make ClienteYA more recognisable as ClienteYA?**
>
> **Or less?**
