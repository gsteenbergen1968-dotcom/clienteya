# A Letter from the Founder

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Approved

**Language:** English (Master Version)

**Last Updated:** June 2026

**Founder:** Gerard Henri Steenbergen

---

# Dear Future Colleague,

If you are reading this, welcome.

Whether you joined ClienteYA as an engineer, designer, marketer, salesperson, customer success specialist or leader...

we are grateful you are here.

Because from this moment on, you are no longer just building software.

You are helping entrepreneurs build better businesses.

---

# Why ClienteYA Exists

ClienteYA was never created because the world needed another CRM.

It was created because we saw something happening over and over again.

Entrepreneurs work incredibly hard.

They care about their customers.

They genuinely want to build lasting relationships.

Yet they lose opportunities every day.

Not because they lack talent.

Not because they lack ambition.

But because it is impossible to remember everything.

Promises are forgotten.

Follow-ups are delayed.

Relationships slowly disappear.

Not through bad intentions.

Through busy lives.

We believed entrepreneurs deserved better.

That belief became ClienteYA.

---

# What We Are Really Building

Some people will say we build software.

Others will say we build Artificial Intelligence.

Some will call us a SaaS company.

They are all partly right.

But none of them describe who we really are.

We are building confidence.

We are building trust.

We are protecting relationships.

Technology is simply the way we do it.

---

# The Decision That Shaped ClienteYA

From the very beginning, we chose to build a company whose principles would always guide its products.

We believed that values should come before features.

We believed that trust should come before growth.

We believed that long-term thinking should always outweigh short-term success.

Those beliefs became the foundation of ClienteYA.

That is why the Constitution of ClienteYA was written before our first official launch.

Not because documents matter more than software.

But because principles matter more than features.

---

# Never Forget This

Entrepreneurs do not wake up thinking about software.

They wake up thinking about their customers.

Their employees.

Their families.

Their responsibilities.

If ClienteYA ever becomes complicated...

we have failed.

If ClienteYA ever becomes impressive but not useful...

we have failed.

If ClienteYA ever stops helping entrepreneurs make better decisions...

we have failed.

Always remember who we serve.

---

# Our Responsibility

Every line of code matters.

Every design decision matters.

Every recommendation matters.

Because somewhere, an entrepreneur will trust ClienteYA to guide an important business decision.

Never take that responsibility lightly.

Trust takes years to build.

Only moments to lose.

---

# Build for the Next Decade

Technology will evolve.

Artificial Intelligence will evolve.

Markets will evolve.

ClienteYA should evolve too.

We hope you improve many things we created.

Innovation is part of our journey.

But while you improve the product...

protect its soul.

Never sacrifice trust for growth.

Never sacrifice simplicity for complexity.

Never sacrifice relationships for data.

Those principles are timeless.

---

# If You Ever Doubt

Whenever you face a difficult decision...

do not ask:

"What is the easiest solution?"

Do not ask:

"What is the fastest solution?"

Do not ask:

"What would our competitors do?"

Instead ask one simple question:

> **"Is this still ClienteYA?"**

If the answer is yes...

continue.

If the answer is no...

stop.

Reconsider.

Protect the identity before changing the product.

That single question has guided us from the very beginning.

We hope it will continue to guide you for many years to come.

---

# Thank You

Thank you for choosing to become part of ClienteYA.

We may never meet.

Our paths may never cross.

But if you continue building this company with integrity, patience and respect for entrepreneurs...

then you are helping fulfill a dream that began long before you arrived.

For that, we are sincerely grateful.

---

# One Final Thought

Companies are remembered for their products.

Great companies are remembered for their principles.

Protect both.

But always protect the principles first.

Because products can be rebuilt.

Trust cannot.

Welcome to ClienteYA.

Take good care of it.

One day, you will pass it on to someone else.

---

With gratitude,
